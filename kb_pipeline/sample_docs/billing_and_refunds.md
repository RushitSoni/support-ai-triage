# Billing and Refunds

## How Billing Works
You're billed automatically on the same day each month, based on your plan's renewal date. Charges appear on your statement as "SUPPORTAI INC."

## Requesting a Refund
We offer refunds under the following conditions:
- You were charged in error (e.g., duplicate charge) — full refund, no questions asked.
- You cancelled within 7 days of a renewal but were still charged — full refund.
- You're unhappy with the service after a new subscription (first 14 days) — full refund, one-time only.

Refunds outside these windows are evaluated case-by-case and require manager approval.

## Duplicate Charges
If you see two charges for the same billing period, this is usually caused by a retried payment after a temporary card decline. The duplicate is refunded automatically within 3-5 business days. If it hasn't been refunded after 5 business days, escalate to a human agent — do not promise a specific refund timeline without checking the payment processor first.

## Cancelling Your Subscription
Go to Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; you keep access until then. There is no early-termination fee.

## Important
Never share full credit card numbers back to a customer in a reply, even if they paste them into a ticket. Redact and confirm only the last 4 digits.
