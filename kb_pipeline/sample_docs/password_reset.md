# Resetting Your Password

## Overview
If you've forgotten your password or are locked out of your account, you can reset it yourself without contacting support.

## Steps to Reset
1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?"
2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link. It expires after 30 minutes.
4. Click the link and choose a new password (minimum 8 characters, at least one number).
5. Log in with your new password.

## Troubleshooting
- If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.
- If your email address isn't recognized, you may have signed up with a different email — check any other addresses you commonly use.
- If you still can't reset your password after trying the above, contact support and we'll manually verify your identity before resetting it.

## Related
- Account lockouts after too many failed login attempts are automatically lifted after 15 minutes.
