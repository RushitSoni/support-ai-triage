# Managing Team Members

## Inviting a New Team Member
1. Go to Workspace Settings → Team.
2. Click "Invite Member" and enter their email address.
3. Choose a role: Admin, Editor, or Viewer.
4. They'll receive an email invite valid for 7 days.

## Roles and Permissions
- **Admin**: full access, including billing and removing other members.
- **Editor**: can create and edit content, cannot access billing.
- **Viewer**: read-only access.

## Removing a Team Member
Only Admins can remove members. Go to Workspace Settings → Team, find the member, and click "Remove." Their access is revoked immediately.

## Seat Limits
Your plan determines how many team members you can add. If you hit your limit, you'll need to upgrade your plan or remove an inactive member before inviting someone new.
